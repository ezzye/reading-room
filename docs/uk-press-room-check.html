Scope and sharing

This pass covers major UK national and leading Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland and London newspaper websites. 6 English regional websites are deferred. It does not cover every UK newspaper.

Two stories are sampled from each accessible website family where observed. Shared daily/Sunday websites are not evidence of independently checked print editions.

A refused homepage does not establish that every article from that paper is unavailable. Existing Times and London Telegraph library routes remain separate from this front-page selection check. An adapter gap is also not evidence that Camden lacks the publication.

Normal Reading Room imports have a separate shared allowance of 30 attempts per UTC day across newspapers, rooms and members. Unsuccessful imports count; opening saved articles does not. It is an app safeguard, not a newspaper or Camden quota. This press audit used a separately reserved operator allowance. The normal counter stores a total, not a per-newspaper reading history.

The report opens offline. Source and saved-reader links need internet access. Save the HTML file and attach that file when sharing.
