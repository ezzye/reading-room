Failed or refused import
Uses zero saved-edition places. Its source/provider requests can still count toward separate protection limits.
Open or reuse a saved edition
Uses zero newly saved-edition places.
Work is in progress
A temporary hold protects a place from concurrent imports. It expires after 15 minutes or at midnight UTC, whichever comes first; no successful save means no completed-edition charge.
Separate request protections
