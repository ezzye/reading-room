Arday article matching

Four dated Arday library headlines have been verified. Three fresh URLs produced private editions with collapsed AI critique. The final 24 July add-to-room test reached the app’s daily import limit. Original images and video remain unresolved.

What changed

Camden searches previously used three broad words and checked only the first twenty results. Newer stories from other publications could crowd out the requested article. Reading Room now tries the complete headline and a bounded search using its key words. Ordinary Next links are followed for at most three pages per query. No guessed field, date or pagination values are submitted.

When publisher metadata is unavailable, words from a dated Telegraph URL can be used as a labelled search hint. A fetched library record must meet a stricter headline score, a known matching date and the publication checks. The saved edition explains that the original web headline was not independently observed. No unrelated article is silently substituted.

Private reading on phones

Open the shelf, choose Add article and paste a source link. Matched text is stored in the room with AI commentary initially closed. The room’s Discussion supports replies; access is controlled by membership. This library lookup uses the existing server connection and does not require Page Lens or a running Mac.

The 31 July, 6 August and 8 August URLs opened new editions without typing a headline. Their discussions are present and AI controls start closed. A critique was opened and closed in the 8 August edition. No new discussion was posted by the testing agent. A new Danielka-phone check is still separate from these desktop UI and server results. The 8 August headline and subtitle were checked after the presentation tidy-up; all eight AI controls remained closed. The 24 July diagnostic matches a record but does not itself test the stricter final URL-intake check. The 30-import limit resets at 00:00 UTC; saved editions still open.

The remaining media gap

No original media have been verified for these Arday editions. The article body and critique are usable, but the requested complete text-plus-media experience has not been achieved.

The returned Camden records are text editions. Exact fonts, page layout, original photographs and videos have not been reconstructed from those records. Reading Room labels its newspaper styling as approximate. Earlier ordinary Telegraph server requests returned HTTP 402, and a clean server browser returned HTTP 403. Those source routes were stopped. Public image/index checks found related material but no assets verified as belonging to the four exact articles; those related images were not inserted.

Text success is not media success. The separate clock-tower story has a previously reviewed original-player link reported to work on a phone. That does not establish media for any Arday article.
