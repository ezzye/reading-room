Reading Room · Optional Chrome tool

Capture directly.
Review in your room.

Page Lens connects directly to Reading Room. The capture path does not require the ChatGPT extension.

1

Enable the article

Open the newspaper in Chrome. In Page Lens, enable direct capture, then confirm the connection in Reading Room.

2

Request from your phone

Keep the enabled newspaper tab visible on the computer. In Reading Room, choose Capture visible media.

3

Review and add

Check the returned images and player links. Choose Add media to attach them to the matching article in the private room.

A short, specific connection

  • The room owner enables one article tab for up to ten minutes.
  • The extension captures visible image and supported player references. It does not move or scroll the page.
  • Only the fixed capture command is accepted. No remote JavaScript, cookies, passwords or Camden details are read.
  • Stop connection ends the session. Expired results cannot be retrieved; stored staging data are cleared when a later connection is created.
  • Nothing is appended to the saved article until the owner reviews and imports it.

Chrome must be open for this optional capture. Ordinary phone article lookup through the server-side Camden connection continues independently of the computer.

What the extension update requests

Direct capture, introduced in Page Lens 0.2.0, adds a background worker and an alarm to check for a capture request every thirty seconds. It adds network access to the exact configured Reading Room address. Newspaper access remains limited to the tab you explicitly enable.

Page Lens 0.2.2 keeps those permissions unchanged. It adds connection recovery, protects against repeated Enable clicks and shows a Check now control with safe diagnostic timestamps. A successful code check is separate from a real Chrome capture and playback on Danielka’s phone. Telegraph’s player may restrict embedding; no stream address is invented.
