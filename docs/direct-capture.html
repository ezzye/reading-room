Reading Room · Optional Chrome tool

Capture directly.
Review in your room.

Page Lens connects directly to Reading Room. The capture path does not require the ChatGPT extension.

1

Enable the article

Open the article in Chrome and choose Enable this tab in Page Lens. In Reading Room, choose Enable and return to article. That local confirmation selects the same enabled tab; it does not change its URL.

2

Request from your phone

From Reading Room, including on your phone, choose Request visible media. Keep that exact article selected in its original, unminimized Chrome window. The capture represents the rendered tab viewport; whether Chrome is the foreground Mac app is not checked.

3

Review and add

Check the returned images and player links. Choose Add media to attach them to the matching article in the private room.

A short, specific connection

  • The room owner enables one article tab for up to ten minutes.
  • Only local pairing confirmation selects the originally enabled article tab. Remote requests never activate tabs, navigate, scroll, dismiss overlays or accept scripts.
  • Capture checks the rendered article viewport, visibility and media hit-testing. It is not a physical-screen screenshot and does not verify OS app focus. Keep the original Chrome window unminimized.
  • Only the fixed capture command is accepted. No remote JavaScript, cookies, passwords or Camden details are read.
  • Stop connection ends the session. Expired results cannot be retrieved; stored staging data are cleared when a later connection is created.
  • Nothing is appended to the saved article until the owner reviews and imports it.

Chrome must be open for this optional capture. Ordinary phone article lookup through the server-side Camden connection continues independently of the computer. If the page blocks ordinary access or requires a subscription, headless or rendered capture is not a bypass; report the access outcome and use an authorised library route where available.

What the extension update requests

Direct capture, introduced in Page Lens 0.2.0, adds a background worker and an alarm to check for a capture request every thirty seconds. It adds network access to the exact configured Reading Room address. Newspaper access remains limited to the tab you explicitly enable.

Page Lens 0.2.2 keeps those permissions unchanged. It adds connection recovery, protects against repeated Enable clicks and shows a Check now control with safe diagnostic timestamps. A successful code check is separate from a real Chrome capture and playback on Danielka’s phone. Telegraph’s player may restrict embedding; no stream address is invented.

Current rendered-viewport behavior (Page Lens 0.2.3)

The local Reading Room confirmation validates the one-use pairing and returns to the exact bound source tab. Once enabled, the phone can request media from that rendered tab viewport. The original window must remain unminimized and the article tab selected. OS foreground-app focus is not verified, so this is not proof of what was physically on screen. Remote commands have no tab activation, navigation or scrolling authority; they run only the bundled extractor. No cookies, hidden paid text, player-stream bypass or arbitrary remote script are requested.
