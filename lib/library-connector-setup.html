RET · Reading Room

Your automatic Camden lookup

A paired Chrome connector on your Mac handles Times article requests using your signed-in Camden NewsBank session. Your daughter pastes a Times URL in RET and receives a private reading edition with collapsed AI critique and an AI answer.

One-time setup is required. The Mac must stay awake with Chrome running. A library sign-in may need renewing. Keep this RET setup tab signed in as the owner. The connector retains the supplied library text in the private group. Original Times styling and media require a matching readable publisher capture; a library-only result uses clearly marked approximate typography.

  1. Download the connector ZIP and unpack it. In Chrome’s Extensions page, choose Developer mode → Load unpacked and select the unpacked folder.
  2. Open Camden’s digital library in Chrome and sign in to NewsBank with your own account. Confirm a Times text article opens.
  3. Sign in as the RET owner below. Generate a one-time pairing code, open the RET Camden Connector icon, and paste the code. Use the same RET address in the extension.

The code expires in ten minutes. Don’t share it with other people.

What is sent and saved

Your borrower number, PIN and browser cookies remain in Chrome. A matched article’s bounded text is sent to private RET and DeepSeek for commentary. RET saves the supplied text, source metadata and separate AI annotations so your invited group can read and discuss it. The normal RET sign-in session is used in its own tab; cookies are never read or exported by the extension.

The extension can capture readable Times article presentation and read Times headlines and Camden NewsBank pages, open temporary lookup tabs, and poll RET once a minute. It does not invent missing media, export your login, or search other library services. Pairing expires after 30 days and can be revoked here.
