How your connection is used

Your borrower number is encrypted on the server and used to sign in to Camden’s NewsBank service. A PIN is not needed. Room members cannot see it, and separate rooms do not inherit it.

Matched article text is kept in this private room and sent to DeepSeek for separate AI critique. Newspaper images, video and exact typography are available only where the source supplies them.
