RET is independent of the publishers. Original reporting stays attributed; AI commentary is clearly separate. Reading access does not automatically include republication rights. The share limit is fifteen new links per page per UTC day, each valid for seven days.

Media requests go directly to their original hosts. Saved HTML keeps text and commentary offline; general imports need internet for remote pictures, fonts and provider video. Exact fonts and video playback are not guaranteed.
